## Hi, I'm António

I'm an MSc student in **Artificial Intelligence** at the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP), currently on an Erasmus+ semester at AGH University of Krakow. I work on machine learning and on getting models into production (ML and MLOps).

- **Now:** my MSc dissertation at LTPlabs, an *agentic AI analytics assistant*. It is an LLM agent that lets business users query, analyse and chart the outputs of decision-support models in natural language, with answers traceable to the source data.
- **Before:** two internships at LTPlabs. I built production-planning optimization systems (Python, PuLP, Gurobi), multi-page Power BI dashboards for clients, and deployment pipelines with Docker, CI/CD and Azure.
- **Interests:** LLM agents and RAG, NLP, learning from data streams, MLOps.
